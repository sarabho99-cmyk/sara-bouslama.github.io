about · 03

A practice of
looking, & listening.

Sara Bouslama is a 26-year-old visual practitioner based in Darmstadt, Germany. She graduated from the Higher Institute of Audiovisual & Film Professions in Visual Studies, then completed a master's in International Media at Hochschule Darmstadt.

Her work spans documentary, brand video, museum communications, and curated editorial — connected by a quiet attention to light, framing, and rhythm.
